# Changelog

Mosaic Crochet Editor is deployed continuously from `master`; changes are grouped by date rather than release version.

## 20 September 2026

- Replaced the welcome screen with About, including the full changelog and New, Open, and Example actions.
- Made the Design tool rail more compact and streamlined workspace guidance.
- Started row numbering with the bottom foundation row.
- Constrained About to a readable height, kept its actions visible, and made only its changelog entries scroll.
- Made Crochet instructions compact, directly selectable, cached, and persist progress across stitch edits.
- Separated Crochet row details, showed yarn colours, wrapped long instructions, and kept inspectors open until the chart changes.

## 19 September 2026

- Made Pattern a modeless inspector with live, undoable edits.
- Combined generated instructions and progress into the stable Crochet workspace.
- Kept one shared canvas and added chart-derived work-in-progress previews.

## 16 September 2026

- Added a keyboard-operated canvas cell cursor.
- Added first-run pattern, file, and example choices.

## 15 September 2026

- Added direct selection modes, editable symmetry guides, and drawing previews.
- Improved touch targets, enlarged-text layouts, and file error reporting.
- Separated stitch-guidance visibility from invalid-placement prevention.

## 14 September 2026

- Improved focus restoration, inspector keyboard behavior, hover labels, and compact-menu navigation.
- Added visible canvas rotation and clearer pattern-file errors.

## 13 September 2026

- Introduced the adaptive editor shell, explicit canvas navigation, and browser recovery status.
- Added versioned project files, browser recovery, and persistent undo history.
- Added repeat transforms and resumable crochet instructions.

## 12 September 2026

- Added touch mask movement, selection symmetry replication, repeat grids, and transform safety bounds.

## May 2026

- Created the browser editor with row and centre-out geometry, drawing tools, overlays, selection, symmetry, saving, and generated crochet instructions.
